## 🌟 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** who thrives on turning complex problems into elegant, scalable solutions. Over the years, I've honed my craft across the full stack — from pixel-perfect frontends to robust, production-grade backends — and I genuinely love every layer of the process.

My core expertise lives in the **MERN ecosystem** (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js), but I've never been one to stay in a single lane. Whether it's architecting **multi-tenant SaaS platforms**, integrating third-party APIs, wiring up real-time communication with **Socket.io**, or handling payments through **Stripe** — I bring the same level of care and depth to every project.

I believe great software isn't just about writing code — it's about understanding the *why* behind the product, collaborating with people who care, and shipping work that makes a real difference.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** A multi-tenant **SaaS platform** with role-based access control, third-party API integrations, and scalable microservice architecture
- 🧠 **Always exploring:** AI-powered features, advanced caching strategies, and cloud-native deployment patterns
- 🤝 **Open to:** Collaborations on challenging full-stack or SaaS projects
- ⚡ **Philosophy:** *"Ship fast, learn faster, and never stop iterating."*
